Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

About this application

Raj Oura Health is a personal wellness application under development for analyzing Oura Ring health and recovery data.

Information accessed

With authorization, the application may access heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, readiness, and related wellness data through the Oura API.

How information is used

Information is used to display historical health trends and personal wellness insights.

Storage and sharing

The initial version is designed to store health data locally on the owner's devices. Health data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data access and deletion

The account owner can revoke application access through Oura's account controls and delete locally stored application data.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
